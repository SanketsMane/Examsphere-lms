Platform Usage Rules
During demos
	•	No personal contact sharing allowed.
	•	Platform chat and video only.
	•	Classes auto-recorded for safety.
After demos
	•	Direct contact unlocked only after paid course purchase.
	•	Payments must stay inside the platform wallet.

Strong Anti-Bypass Controls
	•	Auto-block phone numbers, emails, and social app keywords.
	•	AI chat scan to flag risky messages.
	•	Warnings on first violation.
	•	Account freeze on second violation.
	•	Permanent ban on repeated abuse.

Payment and Trust System
	•	Escrow wallet holds student payment.
	•	Tutor payout released after class completion.
	•	Three-day dispute window for students.

Retention and Growth Features
	•	Monthly learning streak rewards.
	•	Wallet credits for renewals.
	•	Referral rewards locked to platform use only.
	•	Subscription-only flash discounts.

Legal and Onboarding Protection
	•	Mandatory agreement acceptance at signup.
	•	Clear penalties shown before first demo booking.
	•	Logged consent for compliance and disputes.
